Monarch Butterfly

Scientific name

Danaus plexippus

Beautiful wings

Orange wings with bold black veins and delicate white spots along the edges.

Favourite food

Nectar collected from flowers.

Amazing fact

Monarch butterflies are famous for their extraordinary long-distance migrations.
